Teaching Philosophy

What gives teaching meaning to me

Teaching is an opportunity to encourage students to carve their own path. When students truly learn the fundamental concepts in engineering, science, and math, they can explore any course that interests them and go all in. For me, teaching is a chance to build students’ confidence, to help those who feel behind “catch up,” and to show that paths are not linear.

Why it matters that students achieve their learning goals

Fully understanding foundational concepts is essential for succeeding in subsequent courses. As an undergraduate, I experienced firsthand how gaps in understanding can make upper-division courses extremely difficult. Research suggests that students enjoy courses and projects more when they grasp the fundamentals and can apply them: the work they present is deeper, and their satisfaction is higher when they feel they got something useful out of the course.
